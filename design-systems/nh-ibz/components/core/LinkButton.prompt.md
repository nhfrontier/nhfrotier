The brand's quiet text link — "자세히보기 ›". Sits at the bottom of cards or section headers to reveal more.

```jsx
<LinkButton>자세히보기</LinkButton>
<LinkButton arrow="diagonal" tone="primary">바로가기</LinkButton>
```

- `arrow="chevron"` (›) for "more / detail"; `arrow="diagonal"` (↗) for "go to / external".
- Keep labels short: 자세히보기, 바로가기, 전체보기, 더보기.
